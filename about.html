About GigConnect KE

We connect hardworking Kenyans with real gig and job opportunities — from house help and event work to cashier, driving and delivery roles across the country.

🎯 Our mission
Job seekers struggle to find reliable, fast and accessible work, while employers urgently need trusted people to keep things moving. We bridge that gap — simply, quickly and fairly.
⚡ Fast & casual
No corporate CV mines. Listings are short, contact is WhatsApp-first, and most gigs are live in minutes.
🛡️ Safety first
Every listing is reportable, scams are flagged, and we push clear rules — never pay to apply, meet in public first.
📍 Local across Kenya
From Nairobi to Nakuru, Mombasa to Kisumu — we cover towns and neighbourhoods, not just big employers.
