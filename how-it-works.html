Stay safe

Meet in public first, agree terms in writing, and never send money to apply.

🛡️ Safety tips

Never pay to apply
Legitimate gigs never charge applicants. If a poster asks for money upfront, walk away and report it.
Meet in public first
For house help or event work, arrange a first meeting at a public place or with someone you trust.
Guard your ID
Don't share ID documents, bank details, or M-Pesa pins before meeting in person.
Agree terms clearly
Confirm pay, hours, and scope in writing (a simple WhatsApp message works) before you start.
